The name

Kunsei is the Japanese word for smoking and curing

It's the slow transfer of fire into flavour - patience turned into food. We smoke every cut low and slow, and we open Fri - Sun, so nothing leaves the smoker before it's ready.

燻製 Kun·sei - nounthe process of smoking or curing food over time, using smoke rather than speed to build flavour.

Why it tastes like this

Made with time, fire and love

Good food can't be rushed, and it can't be faked. Every cut that goes into our smoker is tended by hand, from lighting the first fire to the moment it's sliced and served. We watch it, we wait on it, and it only leaves the smoker when it's ready.

We cook the way you cook for the people you love. Every rub is mixed by hand, every fire is fed with care, and every plate that leaves our trailer is one we'd be proud to put on our own table. That's what you taste when you eat with us - not just the smoke, but the patience and the heart behind it.

Kunsei was built for the people who gather around it. Every weekend, we light the fire for you.

- Kunsei Smokehouse
