The name

Kunsei is the Japanese word for smoking and curing

It's the slow transfer of fire into flavour - patience turned into food. We smoke every cut low and slow, and we open Fri - Sun, so nothing leaves the smoker before it's ready.

燻製 Kun·sei - nounthe process of smoking or curing food over time, using smoke rather than speed to build flavour.

Why it tastes like this

It starts long before the smoker

You can't shortcut good meat. Every cut that reaches our smoker is A-grade, and our beef comes from animals 18 months old or younger - young enough that the meat stays tender on its own, before the smoke ever gets involved.

That choice costs more and means less margin for error. But it's the difference between meat that needs twelve hours of smoke to be edible, and meat that just gets better and better the longer it sits in it. We'd rather start with the best cut in Witbank and let the fire do the rest, the slow way, the way it's supposed to be done.

- Kunsei Smokehouse
